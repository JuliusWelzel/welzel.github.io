EEGManySteps

A multi-lab collection of gait EEG data to quantify how recording setups and analysis pipelines affect the results.

Started 2024, following the MoBI 2024 conference

Related initiatives EEGManyLabs, EEGManyPipelines

My role Steering committee; website and GitHub organisation; data contributor

Website eegmanysteps.org

Aims

EEGManySteps is a community initiative in mobile brain/body imaging (MoBI). Instead of replicating a single experiment, it collects and harmonises gait EEG recorded with different setups, including existing datasets and new recordings, to identify which acquisition and analysis factors most affect the consistency of gait-related EEG results.

The initiative addresses four research questions:

  1. step-specific time-frequency patterns,
  2. gait-related artefacts across EEG systems,
  3. the influence of preprocessing on EEG results during walking, and
  4. gait-related modulation of secondary-task correlates such as the P3.

Data harmonisation

Contributed datasets are converted to EEG-BIDS and Motion-BIDS and annotated with additional metadata (indoor or outdoor, treadmill or overground, footwear, secondary task, speed protocol, synchronisation method). Sensor locations follow the UNISEP vocabulary, and gait events are standardised. For new recordings, a harmonised auditory oddball paradigm is provided for standing and walking conditions.

My role

Member of the steering committee; co-presenter of the kick-off meeting (September 2024); maintenance of the website and the GitHub organisation; co-author of the project posters at Methods in Mobile EEG 2025 and Psychologie und Gehirn 2025; contribution of a walking dataset together with Nadine Jacobsen.
