Brains, bodies and better science

From treadmill training in Parkinson’s disease to reporting standards for mobile EEG: what I work on, and with whom.

Parkinson’s disease · Mobile EEG · Gait

StepuP

Steps against the burden of Parkinson’s disease. A JPND-funded trial in six countries that records EEG, EMG and motion capture together to find out why treadmill training improves walking in Parkinson’s disease. I lead the Kiel site and run the consortium’s multimodal data pipeline.

JPND · 2023–2026 · shared first author of the protocol

Meta-science · Mobile EEG

META-REP

Within the DFG Priority Programme META-REP, I study the “forking paths” of mobile EEG analysis, with a reporting standard for mobile EEG studies as the cornerstone.

DFG · SPP 2317 · Oldenburg

Gait · Computational motor control

Where to put the next foot

Bayesian integration in foot placement control: how noisy estimates of body state and prior expectations combine into the next step. With Moira van Leeuwen and the Amsterdam group.

VU Amsterdam · ongoing

Open science · Community

IGOR

The Interest Group on Open and Reproducible Research of the DGPs section Biological Psychology and Neuropsychology. I run its BIDS Consultation Hour and co-lead an umbrella review of EEG/ERP preprocessing.

DGPs · since 2020

Mobile EEG · Many labs

EEGManySteps

A community-driven, many-labs initiative testing how hardware, setups and pipelines shape gait-related EEG. I am on the steering committee.
